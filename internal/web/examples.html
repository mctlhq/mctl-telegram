Prompts to try

Twelve prompts you can paste into Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP client once your Telegram account is connected. They are written and reviewed by hand, they are plain prompts rather than installed assistants, and each one lists the tools it uses and whether it can send anything.

Each example is marked either read-only or able to send. A read-only one never sends anything; one that can send says so, and a send still happens only after you confirm it and only if sending is turned on for your account. Nothing on this page reads your chats; the prompts run in your own AI client.

About sending

Every account starts read-only. The one sending example drafts a reply with draft_reply, which sends nothing, and only send_reply sends. It needs the confirmation you give in the conversation, the send scope on your connection, sending turned on for your account and a deployment that allows it. If any of those is missing, nothing is sent. More in the docs and on /security.
