Sending messages

On the hosted deployment at tg.mctl.ai, a newly connected account is read-only by default: send_message and send_media return a preview (sent=false), and the other write tools refuse with an error, so nothing is delivered. Messages are actually delivered only when all of these gates are enabled: the send_enabled flag on your account (your explicit opt-in, made either by choosing Read + send when you connect or later), the telegram:messages:send scope, and the operator-level ALLOW_SEND flag, which is on for tg.mctl.ai. Once all of them are enabled, the service will send real messages to real people on your behalf, and a sent message cannot be unsent. Enable sending only if you accept that.
